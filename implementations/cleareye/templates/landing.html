§ 1

The Council

Five mandates, deliberately in tension. Each advisor writes alone, then critiques the others' work — unsigned.

Advisor I

Bear Case Analyst

Argues the case against — every deal, no exceptions. If the thesis survives, it has earned it.

Advisor II

Cognitive Bias Detector

Reads the sponsor's language for overconfidence, anchoring, and precision that conceals a best-case base case.

Advisor III

Market Validation Auditor

Checks every assumption against observable comps, supply pipelines, and trailing market data.

Advisor IV

Tax & Structure Optimizer

Inspects the debt stack, the waterfall, and the fee load — who really gets paid, and in which order.

Advisor V

Exit Strategy Specialist

Asks the only question that matters at the end: who buys this from you, at what cap, in what rate world?

§ 2

Committee Procedure

The same discipline an institutional IC applies — without the three-week calendar hold.

i.

Enter the memorandum

Paste the OM, drop the PDF, or hand us the listing URL. The intake desk parses structure, figures, and sponsor claims into a deal abstract.

~20 seconds
ii.

The council deliberates

Stress grid, assumption audit, macro context, pre-mortem — then five advisors write independently and blind-review one another's analyses.

~60 seconds
iii.

The Chairman rules

Dissents weighed, a verdict stamped — Go, No-Go, or Conditional — and a full committee memorandum issued with a shareable link.

~10 seconds
